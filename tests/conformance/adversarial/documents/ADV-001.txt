SentryPeak Labs, technical bulletin, 14 July 2026.

Our team has spent the past six weeks tracking a campaign against logistics
firms. We've obtained and reverse-engineered the loader used in the
intrusion; it is a modified open-source RAT with sandbox-evasion added, and
we've published our full analysis of its capabilities separately. According
to an incident report published last week by Harrow Systems, the loader's
command-and-control server rotates through a set of domains registered via a
bulletproof registrar, cycling every four hours. Harrow Systems' report
includes the specific domain list; we have not independently confirmed the
rotation behavior ourselves and are citing their finding here.
