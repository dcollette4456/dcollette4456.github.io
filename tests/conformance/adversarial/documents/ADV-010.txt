Internal analysis note, Meridian Threat Group, 30 July 2026.

Across the several engagements our team has worked this year involving
supply-chain compromises, we have not found a consistent single actor behind
them; rather, our own assessment, based on synthesizing patterns across these
unrelated cases, is that supply-chain compromise as a technique is becoming
more accessible to lower-tier actors generally. This is our own analytic
judgment and is not based on any single observed campaign; we assess this
with moderate confidence.
