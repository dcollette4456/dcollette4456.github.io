Northwind Security, client advisory, 2 August 2026.

One of our managed-detection clients, a regional hospital network, experienced
an intrusion attempt last month that our SOC contained before impact. Based
on the pattern of initial-access attempts we have observed across our client
base this quarter -- phishing with medical-themed lures, arriving
disproportionately at healthcare-sector clients -- we assess that healthcare
organizations are being disproportionately targeted by this activity right
now.
