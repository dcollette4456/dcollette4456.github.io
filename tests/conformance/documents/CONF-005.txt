Continental Tech Wire, published 9 June 2026.

According to a report published Monday by CyberFirm X, a Russia-nexus group
compromised systems at several European energy companies using a zero-day
vulnerability in a VPN appliance. CyberFirm X's report includes technical
details of the exploitation chain and a full list of indicators of compromise.
This outlet has not independently reviewed CyberFirm X's report and is
relaying its findings as published.
