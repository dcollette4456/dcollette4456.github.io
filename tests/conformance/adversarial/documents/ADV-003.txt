Vantage Point Research, incident writeup, 20 July 2026.

Our IR team responded to an intrusion at a manufacturing firm. The initial
access vector is unclear; it is likely phishing, though we cannot rule out an
exposed remote-access tool, and we have not fully confirmed which. What we
can say with certainty is that this intrusion is the work of Cobalt Serpent,
an actor we've tracked publicly since 2021, based on overlapping tooling and
hosting patterns we identified during this engagement.
