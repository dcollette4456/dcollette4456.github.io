Vantage Point Security, Incident Brief VP-2026-0233, published 20 April 2026.

Vantage Point Security's incident response team responded to a business email
compromise engagement at a mid-size freight brokerage in April 2026. During the
engagement, Vantage Point directly recovered the phishing email that initiated
the compromise, the malicious Excel attachment it carried, and the second-stage
payload the attachment's macro downloaded once enabled. Vantage Point analyzed
the payload in its own malware analysis environment and confirmed it functioned
as a remote access tool. This brief publishes the SHA-256 hash of the malicious
attachment, the SHA-256 hash of the second-stage payload, the command-and-control
domain the payload connected to, and two IP addresses observed resolving that
domain during the engagement window.
