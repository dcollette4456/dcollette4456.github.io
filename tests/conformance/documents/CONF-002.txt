Redline Cyber, Field Notes, published 3 May 2026.

Redline Cyber's incident response team responded to an intrusion at a mid-size
logistics firm in March 2026. The attacker gained initial access through a
phishing email and moved laterally using stolen credentials before exfiltrating
data from the firm's file servers. Redline Cyber is withholding technical
indicators, hashes, and infrastructure details at the client's request, and
this notice is intended only to disclose that the engagement occurred and to
describe the attacker's general approach.
