Reading the tags

Every sourced claim carries a tag in the left margin like VND-1. The letters say what kind of source it came from. The number says how much of it you can check for yourself.

GOV government · VND commercial vendor · MED press · TEC catalog or technical record · ACA academic · IND independent researcher · NPO non-profit · ADV the actor itself

1 observed directly and evidenced · 2 observed directly, not enough published to check · 3 observed in the source's own telemetry · 4 relaying someone else's finding · 5 the trail ends somewhere you cannot reach

×2 corroborated means two sources observed it independently, using data neither got from the other. Four outlets repeating one report is still one source. We publish behavior rather than indicator feeds; indicators live in the sources we name.

Full legend and the accumulated record for every source cited: coverage/legend and coverage/sources.

Information classification system

[AM] Ambient: widely distributed information, standard training data, general public knowledge.

[IX] Indexed: publicly available but requires deliberate searching, academic databases, archived materials.

[GT] Gated: behind access restrictions, paywalls, subscriptions, authentication requirements.

[SP] Specialized: requires domain expertise to interpret correctly. Lay interpretation may differ significantly from expert analysis.
